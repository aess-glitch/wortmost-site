Privacy Policy

Your words. Your device.

Effective date: 3 November 2026

1. Scope and developer

This policy describes the Wortmost iOS app and this support website. Wortmost is a German–Russian vocabulary app with local flashcards, free practice and spaced repetition.

Developer responsible for the app: Ekaterina Kreis. Contact and correspondence address are provided in the Impressum.

2. Information stored on your device

Wortmost stores the following information locally in its app container:

  • Deck names and creation dates.
  • German words, Russian translations, optional plural forms and examples, card creation dates and deck associations.
  • Learning progress: next review dates, intervals, correct/incorrect review counters, and review history including date, rating, German word and deck name.
  • Preferences for appearance, haptic feedback and card sorting.
  • Local recovery files and temporary deletion markers used for the short undo period.

This information supports learning, statistics, preferences and recovery. The built-in dictionary and dictionary search work on device. Wortmost does not require an account and does not provide app-level iCloud or CloudKit synchronization.

3. Network use, tracking and advertising

The app has no developer-operated server connection, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It does not automatically send your cards or learning history to the developer. Local learning statistics are not analytics sent to a server.

This does not mean that no data can ever leave your device: file providers, sharing, external links and system services are described below.

4. Export, import and sharing

You can export a JSON backup through the iOS system file interface and choose where to save it. Backups contain decks, cards (including translations, examples and learning progress), review history, creation dates, backup identifiers and an export date. They do not include appearance, haptic or sorting preferences, or the complete built-in dictionary.

JSON backups are readable files; Wortmost does not add password protection or app-level encryption to them. Avoid putting sensitive personal information in cards or sharing backups publicly.

You can choose On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, or another available file provider. A provider may upload, store or synchronize the selected file under its own terms. Importing a file from a cloud provider may require the provider to download it. Access is obtained through the system file picker, not by uploading your library to a Wortmost server.

Import validates the selected file and shows a preview. Restoration requires confirmation and replaces the current decks, cards and review history; it does not merge them. You can also use the system share sheet to share a recovery file with an app or recipient of your choice. The chosen service or recipient then receives the file contents.

You can separately save or share the built-in dictionary JSON with its attribution and license through the system share sheet. This copy contains bundled dictionary resources only, not your decks, cards, preferences or learning history. The file provider or recipient you select handles the files under its own terms.

5. Automatic local recovery copies

Before a confirmed restore, Wortmost creates and verifies a JSON safety copy of the current data inside its own app container. These are not scheduled or automatic cloud backups. The app retains up to five valid automatic recovery copies during cleanup when creating a new copy; invalid files may remain available for inspection.

Recovery files can still contain cards or history that you later remove or replace in the live library.

6. Apple services and external links

Depending on your device settings, iOS may include app data in device backups, including iCloud Backup. This is separate from Wortmost’s local recovery copies and from saving an exported file to iCloud Drive. Apple handles system backups and any system diagnostics you choose to share under its own settings and policies. See Apple’s explanation of iCloud Backup.

The dictionary information screen includes external links to dictionary sources, Creative Commons license documents and GitHub. Opening a link makes your browser connect to the selected website, whose privacy practices apply. Wortmost does not attach your cards or learning history to these links.

7. Retention and your control

You can edit or delete cards and decks in the app. Deleting a card or deck does not delete past review events: review history is retained separately for statistics and may include the old German word and deck name.

Deleting the app through iOS (rather than offloading it) removes its local app container, including local recovery copies. Exported/shared files and device backups are separate: manage or delete them in Files, the receiving service, or your Apple backup settings as appropriate. Removing local data does not recall copies already shared.

The developer cannot remotely access, export or erase your on-device library.

8. Support and privacy requests

Support and privacy contact: wortmost.support@gmail.com. You can use this address for questions about the app, personal data, and deletion of information you voluntarily sent by email.

The feedback options in the app open the system email composer with the support address and a subject. Sending requires your action. Wortmost does not automatically add a message body, card contents, logs, attachments or device information. If email is unavailable, the app shows the address and lets you copy it. Copying writes only the support address to the clipboard.

Requests are handled through Gmail (Google). When you write to this address, your sender address, message and any attachments are processed through that service. This is separate from the app’s local storage: the developer cannot remotely access or erase your on-device library.

Share only what is necessary; a full backup is not required for an initial question. You can request deletion of information you voluntarily sent in support emails by writing to the same address.

Postal correspondence sent to the address in the Impressum is received and forwarded by Dein-Impressum. This service may process sender details and the contents of your correspondence for that purpose. Send only information necessary for your request.

9. This website

These pages use no analytics, cookies set by the site, external fonts, embedded third-party content, or scripts. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives normal website requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from the app’s local learning data. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes

The policy should be updated when the app’s data practices change. English and Russian versions describe the same practices.
